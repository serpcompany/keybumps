FAQ + FAQPage

Questions

Does it work in any app?

Yes. Your words go where your cursor was when you started.

How long can I talk?

Five minutes by default. Choose 10, 15, 30, or 60 minutes, or no limit, in Settings.

What if I pause?

Keep going. Keybumps writes everything down after you stop, so a pause never cuts you off.

Can I find something I dictated last week?

Yes. Dictation History keeps every recording and its text, to search, replay, and copy.

Can I cancel a dictation?

Press Escape while it’s recording or writing.
