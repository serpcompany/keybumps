    Expanded · questions about what it does + FAQPage

    Questions

    What can Keybumps replace?

    A clipboard manager, a text expander, a dictation app, a window manager, a screenshot editor, a launcher, a menu bar timer, and an emoji picker. Turn on the ones you want.

    How is it different from Raycast?

    Keybumps is a focused set of plugins we build ourselves, sold as a one-time purchase. Raycast has a far bigger extension store and AI features. See the full comparison.

    Does Dictation work in any app?

    Yes. Your words go where your cursor was when you started.

    Can I bring my snippets from Alfred?

    Yes. Keybumps imports an Alfred snippets export.

    What do I need to run Keybumps?

    An Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14.2 or later. Translating dictations needs macOS 15.

    Can I move it to a new Mac?

    Yes. Deactivate it on the old Mac, then activate it on the new one.
