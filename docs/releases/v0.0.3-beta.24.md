# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.24

A red dot tells you an update is waiting, and every release's notes are now in Settings.

## Updates

- When a new version is available, a red dot appears on the Keybumps menu bar icon and on **Check for Updates…** in its menu. It stays until the update installs, even if you close the update window.
- Once the update is downloaded, **Check for Updates…** brings back the restart prompt, so you can restart when it suits you.
- Choosing **Skip This Version** clears the dot.

## Settings

- The new **Changelog** page shows your version and update status, with Check now and Automatically check for updates, followed by every release's notes, newest first. It works offline.
- **Version History** in the "You're up to date!" window opens Changelog instead of a web page.
- The update settings moved from General to Changelog.
