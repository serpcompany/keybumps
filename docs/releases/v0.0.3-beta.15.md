# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.15

Meet Timer, the first capability added to Keybumps.

## Timer

- Open the Command Palette, press ⌘6, type a duration such as `5m`, `1h30m`, or `tea 25`, and press Return.
- The soonest timer counts down beside the Keybumps icon, and the Keybumps menu lists all your timers. Click one there to pause or resume it.
- When a timer ends, an alarm stays on screen and rings until you click **Stop** or **Repeat**, or open the Timers tab. It never rings while you're dictating.
- Timers keep counting through sleep and restarts.
- In Settings › Timer you can turn off the ringing, the countdown, or the menu list, or turn Timer off altogether.

## Also

- Settings lists Timer, and any capability added after it, in its own group below the original seven.
- The Hotkeys tab (Shortcut Coach's history) moves to ⌘7.
