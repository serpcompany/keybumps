# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.15

Meet Timer, the first plugin added to Keybumps, and the Emoji Picker, which you turn on in Settings › Plugins.

## Timer

- Open the Command Palette, press ⌘6, type a duration such as `5m`, `1h30m`, or `tea 25`, and press Return.
- The soonest timer counts down beside the Keybumps icon, and the Keybumps menu lists all your timers. Click one there to pause or resume it.
- When a timer ends, an alarm stays on screen and rings until you click **Stop** or **Repeat**, or open the Timers tab. It never rings while you're dictating.
- Timers keep counting through sleep and restarts.
- In Settings › Timer you can turn off the ringing, the countdown, or the menu list, or turn Timer off altogether.

## Emoji Picker

- Turn it on in Settings › Plugins. It ships off.
- Press ⌘7 in the Command Palette to browse recent emoji and every category, or type a name, keyword, or `:shortcode:` such as `thumbs`, `+1`, or `:joy:`.
- Return copies the emoji. ⌘Return pastes it into the app you're using, then puts your clipboard back.
- Pick a skin tone in its Settings page. Recent emoji stay on this Mac.

## Also

- Settings lists Timer, and any plugin added after it, in its own group below the seven that Keybumps ships with.
- Settings › Plugins lists every plugin with its Command Palette tab, its shortcut, and its on/off switch. Click one to open its page.
- Settings opens filling your screen the first time, then keeps the size you leave it at.
- Type "plugins" in Quick Search to open Settings › Plugins, and use its Browse on keybumps.app button to see every plugin on the website.
- The Hotkeys tab (Shortcut Coach's history) moves to ⌘8.
- A plugin you turn off leaves the Command Palette's tab bar.
