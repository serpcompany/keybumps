# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.27

Choose where ScreenGrabs saves your screenshots and recordings, and find your recordings in the ⌘3 tab beside your screenshots.

## Choose where ScreenGrabs saves

- Settings › ScreenGrabs has a **Screenshots folder** and, on macOS 15 or later, a **Recordings folder**, each with **Show in Finder** and **Choose…**. Until you choose one, each is where macOS saves screenshots, or the Desktop, and follows macOS's setting.
- Your ScreenGrabs shortcuts save screenshots in the Screenshots folder. Screenshots you take with macOS's own tools still go where macOS saves them, and ⌘3 lists both.
- New recordings save in the Recordings folder, each in a folder of its own. Until you choose one, that's where macOS saves screenshots, usually the Desktop, instead of Documents › Keybumps › captures. Recordings already in captures stay there, and ⌘3 lists them too.

## New: Recordings in the ⌘3 tab

- The ScreenGrabs tab (⌘3) lists your recordings beside your screenshots, newest first. A recording shows a frame, a play badge, and its length; a recording of all your screens is one tile, marked with how many displays it has.
- Return copies a recording (every display's video). ⌘Return opens it in the recording window, where **Done** (↩) saves any edits and closes, and **Move to Trash** takes Discard's place. With the search field empty, Space opens a recording or a screenshot in Quick Look.
- Delete asks, then moves the recording to the Trash; Clear All leaves recordings. A recording moved or deleted outside Keybumps shows dimmed, "Moved or deleted", until it's back. While it's dimmed, Delete removes it from the tab without asking.
- With Clipboard History off, ⌘3 still lists your recordings and says why your screenshots aren't there. On macOS 14, where ScreenGrabs doesn't record, ⌘3 lists screenshots only, as before.
