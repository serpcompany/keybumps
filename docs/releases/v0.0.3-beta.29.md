# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.29

See your license's year of updates in Settings, find every plugin under one header, and paste with ⌘V, edit with ⌘E, and see a screenshot or recording in full in the ⌘3 tab.

## Your license's year of updates

- A license includes the updates released in the year after you bought it. Nobody's updates have ended: a license bought before 2026-10-19 gets every update released through 2027-10-19. Keybumps never stops working when the year ends, and you keep the last version released in your year.
- Settings › Account shows "Updates through" and the last day. Once that day has passed, it shows "Updates ended" instead, with **Renew Updates…** and **Check Again**.
- A release from after your year shows in Settings › Changelog and in the menu bar's red dot with **Renew Updates…**, instead of Install. Keybumps doesn't download or install it.
- If you install a newer version by hand, it locks until your updates cover it. Settings › Account then offers **Renew Updates…** and **Earlier Versions…**, which opens the releases on keybumps.app/changelog.
- A key Polar reports as expired now reads "Polar says this license key has expired. Contact support@keybumps.app."

## Settings

- The sidebar is now a standard macOS sidebar list: arrow keys and typing move between pages, rows follow System Settings › Appearance › Sidebar icon size, and the Search field is the system's, with ⌘F on macOS 15 or later.
- Settings shows the page's title in the toolbar beside Back and Forward, a "No Results" message when a search finds nothing, and a red count badge on pages that need attention.
- Every plugin sits under one **Plugins** header, below General, Permissions, and Changelog. **All Plugins** is the Plugins page, Quick Search comes first, and the rest follow by name. Click the header to fold the list.
- A turned-off plugin's name is dimmed and its icon faded. Right-click a plugin to **Turn On** or **Turn Off**, or **Open on keybumps.app**, which opens that plugin's page. Turn On is unavailable for a plugin that needs a newer macOS.

## Command Palette keys

- ⌘V pastes the highlighted row, where ⌘P did. ⌘P no longer does anything. In the Translate tab ⌘V stays with the search field, and **Save and Paste** is ⌘Return while you type.
- ⌘E edits: a screenshot or recording in the ⌘3 tab, a Clipboard image while ScreenGrabs is on, and a snippet. ⌘Return still opens a recording.
- Delete (⌫) is the last action in the footer. Deleting a recording is **Move to Trash**, and Recent Items says **Remove**. What each one does, and which ask first, is unchanged.
- Dictation's detail pane has the same buttons and keys as Translate's: Copy, Paste, Transcribe, Translate, Show in Finder, and Delete.

## One click

- In the Emoji tab, one click copies the emoji. In Timers, one click starts, pauses, resumes, or restarts the timer. They only selected before.
- In the ⌘3 tab, one click selects a tile, which shows that tile's actions in the footer. Double-click copies it, and ⌘-click still edits it.

## New: a larger look in the ⌘3 tab

- The ⌘3 tab shows two columns of tiles, and the highlighted screenshot or recording in full beside them. Up and Down move two tiles at a time.
- A screenshot's pane has Copy, Paste, Edit, Quick Look, Show in Finder, and Delete, with its name and its Dimensions, when it was Taken, and Size.
- A recording's pane shows its first frame and has Copy, Paste, Edit, Quick Look, Show in Finder, and Move to Trash, with its Length, Displays, Taken, and Size. A recording that was moved or deleted shows dimmed, with only **Remove**.
- With nothing highlighted, the pane says "Press ↓ or click a screenshot or recording to see it here".
- Hover shows a ring on a tile but doesn't move the highlight in the ScreenGrabs, Dictation, Translate, and Hotkeys tabs.
