# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.29

See your license's year of updates in Settings, find every plugin under one header, paste with ⌘V, and see a screenshot or recording in full in the ⌘3 tab.

## Your license's year of updates

- A license includes the updates released in the year after you bought it. A license bought before 2026-10-19 gets every update released through 2027-10-19. Keybumps never stops working, and you keep the last version from your year.
- Settings › Account shows "Updates through" and the last day. Once it has passed, it shows "Updates ended", with **Renew Updates…** and **Check Again**.
- A release from after your year shows a red dot on the menu bar icon, and Settings › Changelog offers **Renew Updates…**. Keybumps doesn't download or install it.
- A newer version installed by hand locks until your updates cover it, and Settings › Account offers **Renew Updates…** and **Earlier Versions…**.

## Settings

- The sidebar is a standard macOS sidebar: arrow keys move between pages, rows follow your Sidebar icon size, and Search is the system's field, with ⌘F on macOS 15 or later.
- Every plugin sits under one **Plugins** header, which folds, with Quick Search first and the rest by name. Right-click a plugin to **Turn On**, **Turn Off**, or **Open on keybumps.app**.

## Command Palette

- ⌘V pastes the highlighted row, where ⌘P did. In the Translate tab ⌘V stays with the search field, and **Save and Paste** is ⌘Return while you type.
- ⌘E edits a snippet, a screenshot or recording in the ⌘3 tab, and a Clipboard image while ScreenGrabs is on. Delete (⌫) is now the footer's last action.
- Dictation's detail pane shows its actions with their keys: Copy (↵ or ⌘C), Paste (⌘V), Transcribe, Translate, Show in Finder, and Delete (⌫).
- In the Dictation, Translate, Hotkeys, and ⌘3 tabs, a click now only selects, and a double-click chooses. Hover rings the row or tile under the pointer without moving the highlight.
- In the Emoji tab, one click copies the emoji. In Timers, one click starts, pauses, resumes, or restarts the timer.

## New: a larger look in the ⌘3 tab

- The ⌘3 tab shows two columns of tiles, with the highlighted screenshot or recording in full beside them. Double-click copies a tile, and ⌘-click edits it.
- A screenshot's pane has Copy, Paste, Edit, Quick Look, and Delete, plus Show in Finder when it has a saved file, with its Dimensions, when it was Taken, and Size.
- A recording's pane shows its first frame, its Length and Displays, and **Move to Trash**. A recording that was moved or deleted shows dimmed, with only **Remove**.
