# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.18

Dictation is faster: your text arrives sooner after you stop talking, and a new model option makes it about three times faster.

## Faster Dictation

- Keybumps now starts loading your Whisper model as soon as you start talking, instead of after you stop. A dictation after a few quiet minutes no longer waits for the model to load.
- New, and marked **Recommended**: **Whisper Large v3 Turbo (Fast)**, in Settings › Dictation › Transcription model. It's the same Turbo model, run on your Mac's graphics processor instead of its Neural Engine. In our tests, a 7-second dictation was ready in about 0.3 seconds instead of 0.9.
- It also avoids a wait of up to a minute and a half that the other Turbo model can have when macOS clears its prepared copy after a few days unused.
- It's a 574 MB download. The first dictation after you download it takes a little longer while it gets ready; after that, it's fast.
- As before, your recordings and transcripts stay on your Mac.

## Fixed

- The **Recognition language** and **Maximum recording length** menus in Settings › Dictation open again.
