# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.26

Screenshot Tools is now ScreenGrabs: it takes screenshots as before, and records your screen too.

## ScreenGrabs

- Screenshot Tools is now called **ScreenGrabs**. Your screenshot shortcuts, the ⌘3 tab, and Copy new screenshots to the clipboard are as you left them.
- New on macOS 15 or later: **Record Screen** records an area, a window, or a screen, with your voice and the Mac's sound. Press ⇧⌘5, or type "record" in Quick Search. While ScreenGrabs is on, Keybumps uses ⇧⌘5 in place of macOS's screenshot toolbar, as it does ⇧⌘3 and ⇧⌘4; give Record Screen another shortcut in Settings › ScreenGrabs to keep macOS's.
- While you record, a small bar shows the time and lets you pause, draw, mute the microphone or the Mac's sound, restart, discard, or stop. It's never in the video. The time also shows beside the Keybumps icon in the menu bar, with Stop and Pause in its menu.
- When you stop, the recording opens in a big window to play and scrub, with **Discard**, **Copy** (⌘C), and **Save** (↩) in the same bar as the screenshot editor.
- Recordings are kept in Documents › Keybumps › captures. **Show in Finder** in Settings › ScreenGrabs opens the folder.
- Settings › ScreenGrabs is one page: your shortcuts, the permissions it uses, then Screenshots and Recordings. Recordings holds the sound, countdown, shortcuts-on-screen, and click settings.
- ScreenGrabs needs Screen Recording access, for screenshots and recordings alike. Microphone access records your voice, and Input Monitoring shows the shortcuts you press in a recording; both are optional.
- On macOS 14, ScreenGrabs takes screenshots as before, says recording needs macOS 15, and leaves ⇧⌘5 to macOS.

## Dictation

- New, and now the default: **Automatic** in Settings › Dictation › Recognition language. With a multilingual Whisper model, such as Whisper Large v3 Turbo (Fast), Dictation recognizes the language you speak in each recording, English or Japanese for example, and Dictation History shows it. Apple Speech uses your Mac's language, and Whisper Medium English uses English. A language you chose before is kept.
