# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.26

Record your screen, then trim, crop, and blur the recording before you save it. Screenshot Tools is now ScreenGrabs, and a new plugin, Keystrokes, shows the shortcuts you press.

## ScreenGrabs

- Screenshot Tools is now **ScreenGrabs**, and so is its ⌘3 tab. Your screenshot shortcuts and settings are as you left them.
- Clipboard History now keeps your 50 newest screenshots, the ones in the ⌘3 tab, apart from your 50 newest other items, so neither pushes the other out.

## New: Record your screen

On macOS 15 or later, ScreenGrabs records an area, a window, a screen, or all your screens, with your voice and the Mac's sound. It needs the same Screen Recording access as screenshots, and optional Microphone access for your voice. On macOS 14, it takes screenshots as before and leaves ⇧⌘5 to macOS.

- Press ⇧⌘5, or type "record" in Quick Search, for **Record Screen**. ⇧⌘5 now opens it in place of macOS's screenshot toolbar, as ⇧⌘3 and ⇧⌘4 already do; to keep macOS's, give Record Screen another shortcut in Settings › ScreenGrabs.
- The picker opens the way you last recorded. A window recording shows only that window, not its menus or popovers; record an area or the screen to include them.
- A small bar, never in the video, shows the time and lets you pause, draw, mute the microphone or the Mac's sound, restart, discard, or stop. The menu bar shows the time too, with Stop and Pause in its menu.
- Draw with a pen, an arrow, a highlighter, or a rectangle. With Input Monitoring access, the shortcuts you press show in the video, never what you type. Turn on **Highlight clicks** to show each click.
- Esc never ends a recording or its countdown: use the countdown's **Cancel** or the bar's **Discard**.

## Edit a recording

- When you stop, the recording opens in a window to play and scrub, with **Discard**, **Copy** (⌘C), and **Save** (↩). Closing the window saves too.
- **Trim** the ends, or select a part and choose **Cut Section**. Trimmed ends stay on the timeline, dimmed, to drag back; a cut part stays hatched, with **Restore**.
- **Crop** freely or to 16:9 or 1:1, and **Blur** what shouldn't show, for as long as you choose on the timeline.
- Save and Copy write the edited recording in place of the original, and closing the window asks "Save your edits?".
- Recordings are kept in Documents › Keybumps › captures; **Show in Finder** in Settings › ScreenGrabs opens the folder.

## New: Keystrokes

Keystrokes shows the shortcuts you press on screen, for demos and screen sharing. Turn it on in Settings › Plugins; it needs Input Monitoring access.

- It shows only shortcuts, such as ⌘C, unless you choose All keys in Settings › Keystrokes. Nothing shows while you type a password.
- Choose Keycaps or the classic Bezel style, where the keys show, their size, and how long they stay. **Name the action** adds "Copy" beside ⌘C, and **Show clicks too** shows each click.
- **Show Keystrokes** in the Keybumps menu, or Show & Hide Keystrokes in Quick Search or as a shortcut, hides the keys or brings them back without turning the plugin off.

## Dictation

- **Automatic** is the new default Recognition language in Settings › Dictation. With a model Settings lists as English and Japanese, Dictation recognizes the language you speak in each recording, and the Dictation tab (⌘4) shows it. The built-in model and the English-only one can't tell, so Automatic uses your Mac's language, or English, and Settings says which.
- If you never chose a language, Automatic replaces English. A language you chose is kept.
