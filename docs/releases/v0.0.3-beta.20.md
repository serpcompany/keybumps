# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.20

Translation now keeps the translations you save, and can read them aloud.

## Recent translations

- In the Translate tab, press Return to save a translation. The field clears for the next one, and the translation appears at the top of your recent translations.
- ⌘Return saves it and pastes it into the app you were using.
- With the search field empty, the tab lists your last 50 saved translations. Return copies one without adding it to Clipboard History, ⌘Return pastes it, and Delete removes it after asking.
- Click a translation's speaker button to hear it read aloud in a voice for its language. If your Mac has no voice for that language, Keybumps says so.
- Saved translations are kept on your Mac. To remove them all, use **Clear recent translations** in Settings › Translation.

## Fixed

- In the Command Palette, pressing Return to confirm Japanese or Chinese input now finishes your text instead of acting on the palette.
