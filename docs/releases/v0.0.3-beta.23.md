# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.23

After you dictate, your clipboard is the way you left it.

## Dictation

- Dictation still pastes your words where you were typing, then puts back what you had copied, so your next ⌘V pastes that instead of the transcript.
- If you copy something else in the moment after Dictation pastes, your new copy stays.
- To keep the transcript on the clipboard instead, turn off **Put the clipboard back after inserting** in Settings › Dictation › Inserting.
