# Keybumps 0.0.3-beta.28

⇧⌘4 now opens the area you select in the Screenshot Editor, and Quick Search finds a snippet by a word in its text.

## ScreenGrabs

- Screenshot Area is now **Screenshot Area and Edit**, still on ⇧⌘4: select an area, and it opens in the Screenshot Editor, as ⇧⌘3 does with your screens. It goes on the clipboard only when you press **Save** (↩), even with Copy new screenshots on, and Save also keeps an edited copy. Press Esc while you select, and nothing is taken. If you'd changed or cleared Screenshot Area's shortcut in Settings › ScreenGrabs, Screenshot Area and Edit keeps your choice.
- The Screenshot Editor's tools now run Arrow, Text, Draw, Blur, Redact, and the keys 1 to 5 pick them in that order. It opens with Arrow picked, where it used to open on Blur. The letter keys A, T, D, B, and R still work.

## Quick Search

- Type part of a word in a snippet's text, and Quick Search finds it, as the Snippets tab does. Snippets that match by keyword or name are listed above ones that match only by text. Apps and commands still come before snippets, unless you type a snippet's whole keyword, and snippets come before emoji, files, and folders.
- A snippet's row shows its text under its name. A sensitive snippet's text stays hidden and is never searched.
